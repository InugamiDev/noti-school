# Clay revamp critique (Opus lane)

Base: `clay/port` at 1faa96b. Judged from the built app (`vite preview`, port 4791) driven by Playwright Chromium with synthetic `/api/**` fixtures (signed-in admin, one synthetic institution, two synthetic sources, one answered question with a citation), plus signed-out and API-down variants. Screenshots live outside the worktree in `/tmp/critique/shots` (68 route captures, 26 scroll and interaction captures).

## 1. Verdict

The landing hero delivers the brief: one composer, a real cited sample answer, a quiet slate and terracotta palette, and a 3D scene that tells the "one school, its own shelf" story. Below the hero it reads as AI-generated. Eight sections each open with the same left-aligned two-line 44px H2 and grey lede. The "icon tile + bold title + grey sentence" row appears four times (hero trust row, scope list, how cards, role tabs). Then come a three-step card row with mono `01 02 03`, an uneven bento with word art ("Tiếng Việt English" at 36px), and a dark CTA panel with numbered steps beside a form. Every card is the same white 18px card with the same shadow. The copy leans on triads and aphorisms. The signed-in app is weaker than the landing: it runs inside the marketing shell, the answer page never shows the question that was asked, and the account page shows a sign-in form to a signed-in user.

## 5. Do not do

- **Do not add a dark theme now.** The owner decided "Light theme only for now" (CONTEXT.md item 1). Doing it properly doubles the token and contrast verification work for every component and the 3D materials.
- **Do not remove the 3D hero on desktop.** At 1440 it is the one visual that explains per-school isolation. Make it legible (F9) and skip it on phones (F10) instead of deleting it.
- **Do not remove the stock photo.** The owner asked for one. Give it a real size and a caption (F8) instead of keeping it as a thumbnail in a bento.
- **Do not add more photos, illustrations or a second accent colour** to break the sameness. The sameness comes from repeated layout, not from too little decoration. Vary arrangement (F5).
- **Do not add a "verified" or confidence badge** to answers to look more trustworthy. Schema validity is not evidence verification (DESIGN-CONTRACT).
- **Do not invent social proof** (logos, student counts, testimonials, "trusted by" rows) to fill the landing. There are no partners to show.
- **Do not auto-switch the answer language from the typed text** (F29). Offer the switch. A silent switch breaks the persisted-language rule and surprises bilingual students.
- **Do not turn Admin into a KPI dashboard.** It is banned by the anti-generic gate, and there is no reliable data to chart.
- **Do not shrink the H1 to fit 2 lines at 1440.** The 3-line 68px H1 is the LCP element at 192 ms and anchors the page. Fix the H2 uniformity instead.
