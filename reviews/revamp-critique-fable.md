# Clay revamp critique (independent, Fable lane)

Campaign noti-clay-revamp-20261030. Worktree HEAD `1faa96b` on `clay/port`. Judged from the built app (`pnpm --filter @noti/web build`, Vite preview on 127.0.0.1:4791) driven by Chromium 1243 through Playwright, with `/api/**` routed to synthetic fixtures copied from `tests/web/workspace-clay.spec.ts` (role widened to `admin`; budget, feedback, versions and chunk fixtures added so every admin card renders). Screenshots live outside the worktree in `/tmp/fable-critique/shots` (85 captures plus follow-ups). No code was edited. I did not see the other critic's report.

## 1. Verdict

The UI delivers "clean and clay" and most of the quality-of-life brief: the composer, command menu, school picker, loading and no-answer states are real, keyboard-complete and truthful. It does not yet deliver "nice components" in the sense of components that differ by job: every surface is the same 18px white card with the same border and shadow, and the landing is a textbook SaaS template.
The "AI-generated" tells I can point at: seven sections that are all a 44px heading plus a muted lede plus a grid of identical cards; three rows of icon-tile plus bold title plus one-line body (rules, steps, role features, join steps); a numbered 01/02/03 three-card row; a bento grid with a decorative type specimen ("Tiếng Việt English"); a dark CTA band with a form; a FAQ accordion; a proof row of three icon claims under the composer; two-beat slogan copy ("Students ask. Staff check...", "A missing answer is safer than a made-up rule.") and anaphora ("Nothing from the open web, nothing from other schools.").
The stock photo makes this worse rather than better: a generic open-book-and-apple image cropped to 339 by 254 px, floating in a 752 px card beside vertically centred text, is the single most "template" element on the page.
The 3D hero does earn its place: it is the only element that explains the product (one stack per school, pin on the cited line, locked neighbour, empty slot), and it reacts to the citation chips and the school picker. It costs a 135 KB gzip chunk that is fetched at 195 ms on every landing load, including on phones where it sits below the fold.

## 2. Keep

- **Hero composer and example card** (landing, 1440 and 390, light). One bordered group, picker and language inside the bar, round send, "Enter to ask" hint, Vietnamese placeholder switches with the toggle (`lang="vi"` confirmed). Citation chips move the pin in the scene. This is the product in one screen.
- **3D plinth scene** (landing, 1440). Rendered correctly under SwiftShader; reduced motion gives a still frame; the request-scope variant recolours the empty slot and swaps the card to "Your university isn't set up yet". Keep it, but see F18 for load timing.
- **Command menu** (Cmd/Ctrl+K, both viewports). 600 by 520 px dialog, 19 grouped options, 45 px rows, 16px input, typed `rút` filters to the Vietnamese sample; unmatched text falls back to asking. Overlay is a plain 28% ink tint, no blur. Correct.
- **Answer states** (`#/answer`, 1440 and 390). Loading shows real progress text, skeleton and Stop; "Not enough evidence" offers Browse sources, Rephrase and Report; unavailable is a red notice with no answer text and the draft preserved. No "verified" label anywhere.
- **Truthfulness copy**. "Sample handbook, not HCMIU policy" badge, "Demo collection, synthetic examples only", "Nothing is sent from this form yet", "Independent project. Not affiliated with any university." All present and honest. The join form really does refuse to pretend.
- **Contrast and focus**. DOM text audit over landing, answer, sources, history, admin and auth at both viewports found zero text below 4.5:1 (lowest 4.95:1, chip number on clay-soft). Focus rings are a visible 2px terracotta outline on every control I tabbed through (16 stops recorded in the hero).
- **Skeletons** (sources, landing account button). Shape-matched rows, no flicker, account control stays the same box while identity resolves.
- **Mobile sheet and sticky nav** (390). Only the 69 px nav sticks; the pilot notice scrolls away. Sheet traps focus and closes on Escape.

## 4. Top 10 (impact per effort, in order)

1. F5 school picker placement and phone sheet. One prop change, removes the headline cover on both viewports.
2. F12 outage versus signed-out messaging on admin and answer. Small state split, prevents the worst wrong action.
3. F15 colour-scheme meta, theme-color and em dash in the HTML title. Three lines.
4. F7 aside scroll and the "Sign in" button on failed chunk loads.
5. F25 join band: do not ship a filled primary button that sends nothing.

7. F18 defer the scene chunk and cut three font weights.
8. F13 reserve stage and pilot heights to end the phone layout shift.
9. F8 account page shows the sign-in form to signed-in users.
10. F2 plus F19 break the uniform card rhythm on the landing (one section without cards, numbered steps as a list).

## 5. Do not do

- **Do not add a dark theme.** The owner decided light only; the only dark-mode work worth doing is F15 (stop declaring dark in HTML).
- **Do not remove the 3D hero or replace it with the photo.** It is the one element that explains per-school scoping and citation pins; the photo explains nothing.
- **Do not add more icons or badges.** The landing already has 42 Lucide icons and 11 badges; the icon-tile pattern is what reads as generated.
- **Do not introduce a second accent (for example sage on buttons) to add variety.** Variety should come from layout and type, not hue; the single terracotta rule is working.
- **Do not add a hero gradient, glass blur on the command menu overlay, or card hover lifts.** Hover states measured today are flat colour changes (primary to `#8f3818`, chip to clay-soft, nav to sunken) and that restraint is part of what still looks clean.
- **Do not fabricate history titles or school logos.** The history row problem (F9) needs API support; cache only what this tab has already seen.
- **Do not raise body size on phone.** 17px textarea and 16px body with 1.65 line height are right for Vietnamese diacritics.
