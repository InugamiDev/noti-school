Questions

Which universities can use noti.school?
Any university can request access. HCMIU is the first pilot. Each school is set up separately: its source team approves documents before students can ask about them. Request your university.
Can I see another university's documents?
No. Your account belongs to one school, and answers only use that school's approved documents. Documents, questions and feedback stay inside each university.
Can I ask in Vietnamese?
Yes. Choose Tiếng Việt in the question box. English is also available. Answer quality in both languages is still being evaluated against approved sources.
Does it have all of my university's documents?
Not necessarily. Coverage depends on what your school has approved. Check the source library and each document's edition before relying on an answer.
What if there is no supported answer?
Keep your question specific and check the source library. Missing or conflicting evidence produces a limitation, not a guessed rule. Contact the relevant university office for a decision.
Is this an official university service?
No. noti.school is an independent project in development and claims no official affiliation with any university. Confirm important decisions with the original source and the responsible office.
Can I report an incorrect answer?
Use the feedback action on an answer and point to the claim and passage. Don't include student IDs or other personal information.
